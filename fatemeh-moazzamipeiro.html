Fatemeh Moazzamipeiro is an Iranian Ayurveda physician, researcher and educator specializing in Samhita & Maulik Siddhant. Her work focuses on classical Ayurvedic literature, foundational principles, Grahanidosha, irritable bowel syndrome, gut health, preventive healthcare and traditional medical knowledge.

Overview

Moazzamipeiro combines classical textual scholarship with clinical and translational interests in digestive health and preventive Ayurveda. She leads Ananta AyurHeart and its educational initiatives while contributing to academic publishing through JARI.

Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Ananta AyurHeart and Academic Director of Ananta AyurHeart Academy. The Academy supports globally accessible learning in Ayurveda, classical concepts, Bhagavad Gita studies, Sanskrit, integrative wellness and traditional knowledge systems, including multilingual learning across English, Persian, Hindi and Sanskrit.
